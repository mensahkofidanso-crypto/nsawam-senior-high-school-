About Nsawam Senior High School

Nsawam Senior High School is a second-cycle educational institution located in Nsawam in the Eastern Region of Ghana. The school is committed to providing quality education, discipline, skills, and knowledge to help students achieve their academic and future goals.

General Arts

Subjects in the humanities and social sciences.

General Science

Business

Accounting, Business Management and Economics.

Agricultural Science

Agriculture, farming and related areas.

Home Economics

Food, nutrition, clothing and family management.

Visual Arts

Creative art, design and practical skills.
